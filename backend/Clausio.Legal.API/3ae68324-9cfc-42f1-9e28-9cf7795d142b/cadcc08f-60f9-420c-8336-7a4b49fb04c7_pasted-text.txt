Priya Sharma married Rahul Sharma on 10 February 2022. For the first year of marriage, there were no major disputes. Beginning in March 2023, Rahul allegedly started verbally abusing Priya over household expenses and repeatedly demanded money from her parents.

On 18 April 2023, Rahul allegedly slapped Priya during an argument. Priya did not report the incident because she hoped the relationship would improve.

On 25 September 2023, Rahul allegedly assaulted Priya again after she refused to ask her parents for ₹5,00,000. Priya sustained bruises on her left arm and shoulder. She sought medical treatment at City Hospital on the same day.

Between October 2023 and January 2024, Priya claims Rahul repeatedly threatened to divorce her unless she brought additional money from her family.

On 5 February 2024, Priya secretly recorded an audio conversation in which Rahul allegedly admitted demanding money from her parents.

On 10 February 2024, Priya left the matrimonial home and began living with her parents.

On 12 February 2024, she filed a complaint at Andheri Police Station.

An FIR was registered on 15 February 2024.

Priya seeks protection orders, maintenance, compensation for physical and mental harassment, and criminal action against Rahul.
